MARSHMALLOW CHALLENGE

GROUP REFLECTION

So, what we learned about in this activity is it taught us an important lesson about teamwork and decision making. Our group failed to build a stable marshmallow tower because we focused too much on making it tallest instead of making it strong and balanced. Because of this activity, we realized that having a clear plan and prioritizing stability are more important than aiming for height. This helped us understand that success is not just about big goals, but also about smart planning and learning about mistakes.
